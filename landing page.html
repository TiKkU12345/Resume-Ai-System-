Manual screening
  • Keyword filters miss context — "built ML pipelines" won't match "machine learning experience"
  • Quality drops fast past a few dozen resumes

With Rankly

  • Claude writes a rationale for every top-ranked candidate
The pipeline

From job description to ranked shortlist.

01

Resumes → vectors

Every resume is embedded with Sentence-Transformers, capturing skills and experience as semantic meaning, not just text.

02

Score against the JD

The job description is embedded the same way, and each resume is scored on true similarity to what the role needs.

03

Claude explains the rank

Top candidates get a written rationale from Claude — why they fit, not just a number — so recruiters can trust the shortlist.
